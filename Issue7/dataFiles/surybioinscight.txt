After spending the years 1981-1999 at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research in Bombay, Sury was at the Indian Statistical Institute in Bangalore from 1999 until superannuating in June 2025.  Sury is a Fellow of the National Academy of Sciences, India. His areas of research interest are Algebra and Number Theory. At present, he is a ICTS-Visiting Professor at ICTS-TIFR, Bangalore.
